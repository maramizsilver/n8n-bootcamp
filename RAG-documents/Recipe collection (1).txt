1. QUINOA & GRILLED VEGETABLE BOWL
------------------------------------------------
Servings: 2 | Prep: 10 min | Cook: 20 min

Ingredients:
- 1 cup quinoa
- 2 cups water or vegetable broth
- 1 zucchini, sliced
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 small red onion, sliced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/4 cup feta cheese (optional)
- Juice of 1 lemon
- Salt, pepper, and dried oregano to taste

Instructions:
1. Rinse quinoa and cook in water/broth for about 15 minutes until fluffy. Set aside.
2. Toss zucchini, bell pepper, and onion with olive oil, salt, and oregano.
3. Grill or roast vegetables at 200°C (400°F) for 15-20 minutes until tender.
4. Combine quinoa, roasted vegetables, and cherry tomatoes in a bowl.
5. Drizzle with lemon juice, top with feta if desired, and serve warm or cold.

2. BAKED SALMON WITH ASPARAGUS
------------------------------------------------
Servings: 2 | Prep: 5 min | Cook: 15 min

Ingredients:
- 2 salmon fillets (150g each)
- 1 bunch asparagus, trimmed
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 lemon, sliced
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh dill (optional)

Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F).
2. Place salmon and asparagus on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
3. Drizzle with olive oil, sprinkle garlic, salt, and pepper.
4. Top salmon with lemon slices.
5. Bake for 12-15 minutes until salmon flakes easily with a fork.
6. Garnish with fresh dill and serve.

3. LENTIL & VEGETABLE SOUP
------------------------------------------------
Servings: 4 | Prep: 10 min | Cook: 30 min

Ingredients:
- 1 cup dried red lentils, rinsed
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 celery stalks, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can (400g) diced tomatoes
- 6 cups vegetable broth
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh parsley for garnish

Instructions:
1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Sauté onion, carrots, celery, and garlic for 5 minutes.
2. Add cumin and turmeric, stir for 1 minute.
3. Add lentils, diced tomatoes, and vegetable broth.
4. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 20-25 minutes until lentils are tender.
5. Season with salt and pepper. Garnish with parsley and serve hot.

4. GREEK YOGURT CHICKEN SALAD
------------------------------------------------
Servings: 2 | Prep: 15 min | No cook (if using pre-cooked chicken)

Ingredients:
- 2 cups cooked chicken breast, shredded or diced
- 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 celery stalk, diced
- 1/4 red onion, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Mixed greens for serving

Instructions:
1. In a bowl, mix Greek yogurt, Dijon mustard, and lemon juice.
2. Add chicken, celery, and red onion. Mix well.
3. Season with salt and pepper.
4. Serve over a bed of mixed greens or in a whole-grain wrap.

5. OVERNIGHT OATS WITH BERRIES
------------------------------------------------
Servings: 1 | Prep: 5 min | Chill: overnight

Ingredients:
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/2 cup milk (dairy or plant-based)
- 1/4 cup Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- 1 tsp honey or maple syrup
- 1/2 cup mixed berries (fresh or frozen)
- A handful of nuts (almonds, walnuts) for topping

Instructions:
1. In a jar or container, combine oats, milk, yogurt, chia seeds, and honey.
2. Stir well, cover, and refrigerate overnight (at least 4 hours).
3. In the morning, top with berries and nuts before serving.

6. STIR-FRIED TOFU WITH BROCCOLI
------------------------------------------------
Servings: 2 | Prep: 10 min | Cook: 15 min

Ingredients:
- 200g firm tofu, cubed
- 2 cups broccoli florets
- 1 carrot, sliced
- 2 tbsp soy sauce (low sodium)
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- Sesame seeds for garnish

Instructions:
1. Press tofu to remove excess water, then cube it.
2. Heat vegetable oil in a wok or large pan over medium-high heat. Add tofu and cook until golden, about 5-6 minutes. Remove and set aside.
3. In the same pan, add garlic and ginger, sauté for 30 seconds.
4. Add broccoli and carrot, stir-fry for 5-6 minutes until tender-crisp.
5. Return tofu to the pan. Add soy sauce, sesame oil, and rice vinegar. Toss to combine.
6. Garnish with sesame seeds and serve with brown rice or quinoa.

7. SWEET POTATO & BLACK BEAN TACOS
------------------------------------------------
Servings: 3 | Prep: 10 min | Cook: 25 min

Ingredients:
- 2 medium sweet potatoes, diced
- 1 can (400g) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- 6 small corn or whole-wheat tortillas
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
- Lime wedges for serving

Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Toss sweet potatoes with olive oil, chili powder, and cumin.
2. Roast for 20-25 minutes, stirring halfway, until tender and slightly caramelized.
3. Warm black beans in a small pan.
4. Fill tortillas with roasted sweet potatoes and black beans.
5. Top with avocado slices and cilantro. Serve with lime wedges.

8. GREEN SMOOTHIE
------------------------------------------------
Servings: 1 | Prep: 5 min | No cook

Ingredients:
- 1 cup spinach
- 1/2 banana
- 1/2 cup pineapple chunks (fresh or frozen)
- 1 tbsp chia seeds or flaxseed
- 1 cup almond milk (or milk of choice)
- Ice cubes (optional)

Instructions:
1. Add all ingredients to a blender.
2. Blend until smooth, adding more liquid if needed.
3. Pour into a glass and enjoy immediately.

========================================
GENERAL HEALTHY EATING TIPS
========================================
- Fill half your plate with vegetables at every meal.
- Choose whole grains over refined grains when possible.
- Prioritize lean proteins: fish, poultry, legumes, tofu.
- Use healthy fats in moderation: olive oil, avocado, nuts.
- Limit added sugars and highly processed foods.
- Stay hydrated with water throughout the day.
- Plan and prep meals ahead to avoid last-minute unhealthy choices.
